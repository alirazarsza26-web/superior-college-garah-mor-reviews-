Honest feedback.
Practical improvement.

This independent student-feedback concept is designed to help students make informed decisions and help the college understand recurring campus concerns. Feedback should be based on personal experience, written respectfully, and reviewed fairly. College management may respond, but criticism should not be removed simply because it is negative.
