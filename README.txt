SUPERIOR COLLEGE GARAH MOR REVIEWS — FRONT-END PROTOTYPE

Open index.html in a modern browser to preview the website.

Included:
- Responsive dark-teal and white design
- Supplied Superior College Boys Campus, Garah Mor photograph
- English / Urdu language toggle with RTL layout
- Class/program filters and 1–5 star reviews
- Review submission with pending moderation
- Classroom/facility issue reporting (class, category, location, details)
- Demo management view with issue list and review approval/rejection
- Privacy-minded public display: student names are not requested or shown

IMPORTANT LIMITATION:
This is a browser-only prototype. Submissions are stored in that browser's localStorage.
They do NOT reach the principal, do NOT synchronize between students' devices, and are
not protected by a real admin login. Do not use this demo for real confidential complaints.

Before public launch, connect:
1. A secure hosted database and server-side API.
2. Separate student submission and protected management roles.
3. Secure admin authentication, access controls, backups and audit logs.
4. Moderation, abuse-reporting and retention policies.
5. Privacy notice explaining technical logs and realistic anonymity limits.

No real reviews or ratings are included. All counters begin at zero.
The site is an independent student feedback concept, not an official college website.
